Discover the Art of Sound

At JS Music, we believe that every individual carries a unique musical voice waiting to be unlocked. Founded with a passion for nurturing talent, our academy has been a cornerstone of musical excellence for over two decades.

Our Piano program takes students on a transformative journey — from understanding the fundamentals of finger placement, posture, and reading sheet music, to mastering complex classical compositions by Beethoven, Chopin, and Debussy. Whether you dream of performing at recitals or simply playing your favourite melodies, our structured curriculum adapts to your pace and aspirations.

For aspiring Guitar enthusiasts, we offer a comprehensive program that covers acoustic, classical, and electric guitar. Students learn chord progressions, fingerpicking patterns, strumming techniques, scales, and music theory — all while building the confidence to perform solo or in an ensemble.

Our Keyboard & Synthesizer program bridges the gap between classical training and modern music production. Students explore sound synthesis, digital composition, and contemporary performance techniques that prepare them for the evolving landscape of music.

We combine time-honoured classical foundations with modern, dynamic teaching methodologies. Our state-of-the-art studio provides the perfect environment for your talent to flourish — equipped with grand pianos, professional-grade keyboards, acoustic treatment, and recording capabilities.

Meet Our Master Tutor

Satheesh CC

Founder & Principal Instructor

Satheesh CC is a classically trained musician and passionate educator with over 25 years of experience in teaching piano, keyboard, and guitar. Having trained under master musicians and performed across numerous stages, Satheesh founded JS Music with a singular vision: to make world-class music education accessible and deeply personal.

His teaching philosophy centres around patience, creativity, and fostering a lifelong love for music. Each student receives individualised attention, ensuring they not only learn technique but also develop their own unique musical identity.
